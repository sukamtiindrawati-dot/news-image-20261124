One image that sums up the world's news of **Tuesday 24 November 2026**, drawn as a
newspaper front page: a masthead with the date, one lead story, six more stories with
their own illustrations, and a footer strip of the day's other news.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# writes out/entry.png (1024x1024 PNG)
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`, so both runs write identical bytes. No network is used.

## The stories

All seven were first reported on 24 November 2026, and each is stated as reported —
attributed where the claim was unconfirmed.

1. **Lead — Strait of Hormuz: four tankers hit mines.** Iran says four tankers struck
   naval mines and caught fire; its Revolutionary Guard said it also hit a container
   ship. A maritime centre said the mine claims were unconfirmed.
2. **United States — Huawei found guilty in US trial.** A jury convicts the firm of
   stealing trade secrets and bank fraud on 10 of 12 counts, and clears it of
   racketeering.
3. **Ukraine — Ukraine strikes Russian ships.** Kyiv hits the cargo ship Sparta II in
   the Gulf of Finland and a corvette off Libya.
4. **Saudi Arabia — Drone hits Riyadh's tallest tower.** At least 11 people are wounded
   at the PIF Tower, struck for the second time in five days. Houthi fighters close
   on Aden.
5. **Gaza — Israel kills Hamas security chief.** Naeem Abu Nahel dies in a Gaza City
   strike; seven people are killed in all, despite the ceasefire.
6. **China — Shenzhou-24 lifts off.** China launches two Chinese astronauts and its
   first Pakistani crewmate to the Tiangong space station.
7. **Colombia — 25 hostages held in Cartagena.** Gunmen seize a supermarket in the
   tourist city; five older women are freed as troops move in.

Footer: NATO vows to defend against space threats; Indonesia names two candidates for
vice-president; Mexico to try 'El Chapo Isidro' after US drone help; Hanoi restaurant
blast kills six.
